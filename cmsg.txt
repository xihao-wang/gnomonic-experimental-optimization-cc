feat: isolate evaluation results by YOLO model and switch to yolo12x

Results are now stored under OUTPUT_DIR/<model_label>/ (e.g. yolov8m/,
yolo12x/) so that runs with different models never mix. Both
run_single_config.py and run_comparator.py derive the model label from
Path(METRICS_EVALUATION.YOLO_MODEL).stem automatically.

COMPARATOR.OUTPUT_DIR and COMPARATOR.COMPARISON_OUT_DIR removed from
config — directories are always derived from METRICS_EVALUATION, keeping
the two runners in sync without manual coordination.

Active model switched from yolov8m to yolo12x (largest available).

  evaluation/run_single_config.py   (inject model_label in output path)
