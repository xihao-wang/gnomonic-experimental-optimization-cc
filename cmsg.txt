feat: add cross-model comparator with per-model head-to-head ranking

CrossModelComparator now produces per_model_head_to_head.txt alongside
the existing overall_winner.txt. For each YOLO model and each dataset,
all projection configs are ranked best-to-worst across all metrics
(AP@[0.30:0.95], AP@[0.50:0.95], AP@0.50, AP@0.75, Precision@0.50,
Recall@0.50, F1@0.50, FPS), with delta from rank-1 shown per entry.
A consistency matrix at the bottom reports WIN/LOSS per model per dataset
and the total count of models where the leading config wins consistently.
