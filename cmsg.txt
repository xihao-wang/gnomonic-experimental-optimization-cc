feat: add flexible per-projection config with extra_projections and TEST#28

Introduces resolve_proj_list() as a shared helper that builds the ordered
per-slot projection list from a config dict. Supports two modes:
- Uniform (existing): all projections share latitude/FOV, spaced by lon_step
- Mixed (new): extra_projections list with per-slot longitude/latitude/FOV
  and optional pos:[row,col] for grid placement; remaining slots filled
  uniformly. Backward-compatible with all existing configs.

TEST#28: 1 nadir projection (lat=0, 90x90 FOV, slot [0,0]) + 5 peripheral
projections at lat=45, 60x85 FOV — hypothesis: dedicated nadir sub-view
improves recall for pedestrians directly under the camera.
